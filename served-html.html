Instructions

1. Select board size (11×11 or 15×15) from the dropdown above.

2. Fill out the game board by tapping a cell, then choosing a letter from the popup.

3. Input your seven letters into the letter rack below the board.

4. Click "Go" to see the best possible move!

5. Click "Keep" to save the move or "Discard" to remove it.

Board Editor: Click "🎨 Edit Board" to customize bonus tile positions.

Delete: Tap a cell, then tap the delete button (🗑️) in the letter picker.

Clear: Click "Clear" to reset everything.
